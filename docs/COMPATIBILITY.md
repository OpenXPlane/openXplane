# Compatibility estimate

Updated 2026-10-13. **About 41% implemented, about 28% verified identical to the original.**

A self-assessed rubric, not a measurement. Each subsystem has a weight (weights sum to 100). 'implemented' is the share of that subsystem's work that exists in any form, including approximations. 'verified' is the share whose behaviour has been confirmed identical to the original (bit for bit against its machine code, or exact by the loader/table layout in its code). The overall numbers are the weighted sums. The weights and shares are judgement calls and will change as work and understanding grow. The 2026-10-13 review raises the flight-model estimate from 76%/67% to 80%/71% for the recovered delta branch, full-element force composition, wing predicates, bounds writers and ordered grouping. These four-point changes are conservative judgement calls, not percentages derived from test counts. Kernel comparisons with controlled inputs, recorded CRT results or tolerances do not establish whole-simulator equivalence. The subsequent loader-fixture review keeps estimates unchanged: completed original-loader runs replace partial snapshots, but the current ACF object comparison is diagnostic, does not assert parity, and is explicitly ignored unless requested with local reference aircraft. The chord-loader review also keeps shares unchanged: one newly exact loader loop and its live inputs do not establish complete ACF loading or wing-coordinate parity. The boundary-writer review raises the flight-model rubric from 80%/71% to 81%/72%: the complete rib-coordinate loop and point transform are recovered and the live model now supplies their static ACF/CG inputs. This one-point change is a conservative judgement about a recovered geometry stage, not a percentage computed from 4293 compared words; dynamic state, curvature setup and whole trajectories remain unverified. The curvature-setup review keeps the rubric unchanged: the setup kernel has local original-code evidence, but its complete bounds input and live integration are still missing. Its local corpus is not distributed; the corresponding test is explicitly ignored unless requested. The body/gear reference-point review leaves estimates unchanged: two point-list producers now have exact local original-code comparisons, but runtime body grid preparation, propeller points and the complete reference-vector integration remain unfinished. Their original-code corpora are local and their comparisons are explicitly ignored in ordinary CI. The propeller reference-point review keeps estimates unchanged: the last identified point producer has local exact original-code evidence, but combined runtime state preparation and live reference-vector integration are not yet verified. The combined reference-geometry review keeps estimates unchanged: the ordered gear/wing/body/propeller vector and its final bounds now match a single original-code replay on explicit synthetic runtime state, but real-aircraft geometry preparation, auxiliary outputs and live integration remain unverified. Only allocator operations are replaced in that replay; its corpus stays local. The body-grid loader review raises the ACF rubric from 30%/10% to 32%/12% for the recovered complete grid conversion and strict checks on three actual aircraft. These two-point changes are conservative judgement, not percentages computed from coordinate counts. Full loader parity and UI/graphics parity remain unproven.
